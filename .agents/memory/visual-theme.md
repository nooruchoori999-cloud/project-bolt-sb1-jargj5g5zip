---
name: Visual theme
description: The intended visual direction for TreadFi Learn across future pages and components.
---

Use a warm orange-led, dark-first visual system that feels professional, educational, and appropriate for long-form trading content.

**Why:** The product should reflect Tread's orange identity while remaining comfortable for lessons, tutorials, and dense research rather than feeling promotional or overly bright.

**How to apply:** Build new UI from the shared semantic color tokens and existing orange gradient utilities. Favor warm ink surfaces, high text contrast, clear typographic hierarchy, restrained motion, and readable article widths.
